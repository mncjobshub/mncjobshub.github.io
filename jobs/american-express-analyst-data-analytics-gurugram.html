Job Description

American Express is hiring an Analyst in its Data Analytics function for the Client Hierarchy Centre of Excellence (COE) in Gurugram. This team focuses on understanding what global commercial clients need, advising them and delivering accurate, compliant data. It works closely with clients, strategic partners and many internal teams, including client management, implementation, onboarding, account consultancy, servicing, new accounts, technology and operational excellence. The person in this role will help solve a major client pain point and drive the adoption of digital products with stronger data capabilities.

The job is analytics-heavy. You will analyse complex datasets to spot trends and insights, build dashboards and reports in tools such as Power BI, Tableau or Looker, and write clean SQL queries and Python or R scripts. You will also manage data-cleansing projects for commercial programs, resolve client queries about data, and review client relationships at obligor level, using external sources such as LexisNexis, D&B reports and Secretary of State records to verify legal entities.

Please note that this is an experienced-hire position, not a fresher opening. The working hours are Monday to Friday, 2:30 PM to 1:00 AM IST in a hybrid model. Freshers can still use this listing to understand what skills MNC analytics teams expect at the next career level.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead data-cleansing projects for commercial programs together with client managers, administrators and technology teams.
  • Resolve client data queries with a strong customer-first approach.
  • Analyse trends and flag opportunities for a better customer experience and more digitisation.
  • Verify legal entities using external data sources and public tools.
  • Build reports, dashboards and data visualisations, and present insights to client managers.
  • Support accurate calculation of incentives with internal stakeholders.
  • Deliver high-quality work on tight timelines with minimal supervision.

Eligibility & Requirements

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree with 5+ years of analytical experience in a US MNC, consulting or banking; overall performance rating of 5 or above in the last review

  • Graduate (Bachelor's degree) with 5+ years of analytical experience in a US MNC, consulting firm or bank.
  • Strong SQL and at least one programming language, with Python preferred.
  • Experience with BI tools such as Power BI or Tableau.
  • Ability to explain complex data insights to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Willingness to learn web-based database platforms, AS400 mainframe screens and MySQL-based tools.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication, and attention to detail.
  • Overall performance rating of 5 or above in the last review.
