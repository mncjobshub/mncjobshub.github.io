Job Description

Accenture is hiring a Technology Architect at the Technology Architecture Associate level for its Bengaluru office. The role suits candidates with 0 to 2 years of experience. A Technology Architect designs and delivers the technology architecture for a platform, product or client engagement, and defines solutions that meet performance, capability and scalability needs. The main requirement is proficiency in Azure Stack, and knowledge of Microsoft Windows Server administration and Linux is a good addition.

In this position you are expected to build your knowledge, support the team and take part in problem-solving discussions. You will work with cross-functional teams to collect requirements and turn them into architectural solutions, help document designs and technical standards, and contribute ideas that make the architecture more efficient. As you gain experience you will also guide junior colleagues on architectural best practices.

The posting expects a good understanding of cloud infrastructure and hybrid cloud environments, the ability to analyse requirements and design scalable and secure architectures, familiarity with deploying and managing cloud-based solutions, and experience in troubleshooting and optimising technology platforms. If you are a fresher, a good way to prepare is to build a small hands-on lab with Azure or a hybrid setup and be ready to explain what you built and why.

Key Responsibilities

  • Build knowledge and support the team in delivering architecture work.
  • Take part in problem-solving discussions.
  • Gather requirements with cross-functional teams and translate them into architectural solutions.
  • Help document architecture designs and maintain technical standards.
  • Suggest improvements that increase architecture efficiency.
  • Support junior team members with guidance on best practices.

Eligibility & Requirements

Eligibility: 15 years of full-time education (as stated by Accenture)

  • Proficiency in Azure Stack (must have).
  • Experience with Linux and Microsoft Windows Server administration (good to have).
  • Strong understanding of cloud infrastructure and hybrid cloud environments.
  • Ability to analyse system requirements and design scalable, secure architectures.
  • Familiarity with deploying and managing cloud-based solutions.
  • 15 years of full-time education.
