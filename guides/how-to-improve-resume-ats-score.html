How to Improve Your Resume ATS Score: A Practical Guide for Freshers

Published 25 Nov 2026 by MNC JobsHub

Home › Guides

Most large companies use an applicant tracking system (ATS) to collect and organise applications. A resume that the system cannot read properly can be missed, even when you are a good fit. This guide explains what to do about it in simple steps.

What an ATS actually does

An ATS reads your resume file, pulls out details such as your name, contact information, education, skills and experience, and stores them in a searchable profile. Recruiters then search or filter these profiles. How much ranking or scoring happens depends on the company and the tool, so no one can promise a guaranteed result. What you can control is making your resume easy to read and relevant to the job.

1. Use a simple, single-column layout

  • Keep one column. Multi-column layouts, tables and text boxes can scramble the reading order.
  • Avoid putting important details inside images, icons, headers or footers.
  • Use a standard font such as Arial, Calibri or Times New Roman in a readable size.

2. Use standard section headings

Use clear headings such as Summary, Skills, Education, Projects, Experience and Certifications. Creative headings like "My Journey" are harder for software to recognise.

3. Match your resume to the job description

  • Read the job description and note the tools and skills it names, for example SQL, Power BI or Python.
  • If you really have those skills, use the same words in your Skills section and project descriptions.
  • Do not list skills you do not have. Interviewers will test them.

4. Write results, not just duties

Start each point with an action verb and add a result where you can. For example: "Built a Power BI dashboard that tracked weekly sales across 5 regions" is stronger than "Worked on dashboards".

5. Choose the right file format

  • Submit a PDF or DOCX as requested in the application. Many systems read both, but follow the instructions on the form.
  • Make sure the text in your PDF can be selected and copied. A scanned image of a resume cannot be read as text.
  • Name the file clearly, for example FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf.

6. Keep it short and clean

Freshers should aim for one page. Put contact details at the top in plain text: name, phone, email and a LinkedIn or GitHub link.

Quick checklist before you apply

  • Single column, no tables or text boxes
  • Standard headings
  • Skills from the job description (only those you have)
  • Projects with tools used and results
  • Text selectable in the PDF
  • No spelling mistakes, and consistent date formats
