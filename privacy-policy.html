Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Your privacy matters to us. Please read how this website works.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MNC JobsHub ("we", "us", "our"), accessible at our website, collects, uses and protects information when you visit our pages. By using this website you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account to browse jobs. We may collect the following limited information:

  • Information you give us: your name, email address and message if you use our contact form or email us. We use it only to reply to you.
  • Automatically collected information: like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics or advertising partners may log your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring page and date/time of visit.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website and its content.
  • To respond to your questions, corrections and feedback.
  • To understand which pages are useful and to prevent spam and abuse.
  • To show relevant advertising that helps keep this website free.

3. Cookies and web beacons

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our third-party partners may use cookies to remember preferences, measure traffic and serve advertisements. You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work as expected.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display ads on this website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

We do not control the cookies used by third-party advertisers and recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

5. Affiliate links and sponsored content

Some pages may contain affiliate links to products or services such as resume builders, online courses or practice platforms. If you click a link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not influence which jobs we publish. Such links are marked as sponsored where applicable.

6. Third-party links and job information

Our job posts contain links to external websites, including company career pages. We have no control over the content, privacy practices or availability of those sites and are not responsible for them. Please review their policies before sharing personal information or uploading your resume.

Company logos on job listings are loaded from third-party icon services (such as Google's favicon service and DuckDuckGo). When a logo is displayed, your browser requests it directly from those services, which may receive your IP address and browser details.

7. Social media

We share updates on Telegram and Instagram. When you visit those platforms, their own privacy policies apply. We do not receive your personal data from them unless you contact us directly.

8. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we handle. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Messages sent through our contact form are kept only as long as needed to respond and for record keeping.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, email us at mncjobshub.official@gmail.com. For visitors in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, advertising cookies are used only in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

10. Children's privacy

This website is intended for job seekers aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Disclaimer

  • Information only: MNC JobsHub is an information portal. We are not a recruitment agency, consultancy or employer, and we are not affiliated with any company listed unless explicitly stated.
  • No guarantee: We do not guarantee any job, interview call or selection. All hiring decisions are made solely by the respective companies.
  • Accuracy: We try to keep information accurate and up to date, but job details, eligibility and last dates may change without notice. Always confirm details on the company's official website before applying.
  • No fees: We never charge candidates and never ask for money, bank details or OTPs. If anyone claims to represent us and asks for payment, please do not pay and report it to us immediately.
  • Trademarks: Company names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only for identification and informational purposes.
  • Your responsibility: Candidates should verify every opportunity and take care when sharing personal documents. We are not liable for any loss or damage arising from the use of information on this website or from dealings with third parties.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us or write to mncjobshub.official@gmail.com.
