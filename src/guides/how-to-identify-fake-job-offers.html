title: How to Identify Fake Job Offers and Recruitment Scams in India description: Red flags of fake job offers and recruitment scams, how to verify a genuine job on the company's official careers page, and what to do if you have been cheated. date: 2026-11-25 ---

Freshers are a common target for fake job offers. Scammers copy a company's name and promise a quick job in return for money. These simple checks help you stay safe.

Red flags

  • They ask for money. Genuine employers do not charge for registration, training, a laptop, a security deposit or "document verification".
  • The offer comes without an interview. A real offer follows a proper selection process.
  • Messages come from a personal email or phone number, such as a free email address, instead of the company's own domain.
  • Urgency and pressure. "Pay today or lose the seat" is a classic scam line.
  • Unrealistic salary for a fresher role, or "guaranteed placement".
  • Poor spelling and an unprofessional offer letter, or a letter that asks you to pay before joining.
  • Chats only on messaging apps with no official interview link or recruiter profile you can verify.

How to verify a job

  1. Open the company's official website by typing its address yourself, not through a link in a message.
  2. Find the same role on its careers page and apply there.
  3. Check that the recruiter's email address uses the company's own domain.
  4. Search the company name with the word "scam" or "fraud" to see if others have reported it.
  5. Ask a friend or senior who works in the industry if the process looks normal.

Protect your information

  • Do not share your bank details, OTP, PAN or Aadhaar numbers during an application before a verified offer.
  • Never pay "processing fees" to anyone claiming to hire for a company.
  • Keep your resume free of unnecessary personal details such as your full home address.

If you have been cheated

  • Stop all communication and keep screenshots, payment receipts and numbers.
  • Report it on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national cybercrime helpline 1930.
  • Inform your bank immediately if you made a payment.
  • Tell the company whose name was used, using the contact details on its official website.
