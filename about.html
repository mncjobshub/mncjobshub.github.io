About Us

Helping freshers and off-campus candidates find genuine MNC opportunities.

Who we are

MNC JobsHub is an independent job update portal built for fresh graduates, final-year students and off-campus candidates in India. We collect hiring announcements from top technology companies and MNCs, organise them in a simple format and share them for free so that candidates can spend less time searching and more time preparing.

Our mission

Most freshers struggle with scattered information, outdated links and fake job posts that ask for money. Our mission is to make the first step of a career easier by publishing clear, accurate and timely job information: who can apply, what the role involves, what the salary range looks like and where to apply officially.

What we publish

  • Off-campus drives and fresher openings from MNCs and product companies.
  • Internships and training programmes for students and recent graduates.
  • Roles in software development, data analytics, testing and QA, cloud and IT support.
  • Eligibility criteria, batch details, selection process and official apply links.

How we verify jobs

Before a job goes live, our team checks that it is listed on the company's official careers page or an official announcement. We review the batch, eligibility and last date, and we write our own summary of the role instead of copying text. If a posting looks suspicious or asks candidates for a registration or training fee, we do not publish it. If a job is later closed or found to be incorrect, we mark it as expired or remove it.

What we never do

  • We never charge candidates for any job, interview or referral.
  • We never guarantee a job or selection. Hiring decisions are made only by the respective companies.
  • We never ask for your bank details, OTPs or personal documents.

How we sustain this website

The website is free to use. To cover hosting and content costs we display advertisements and may include affiliate links to career-related resources such as resume tools and interview preparation courses. These never influence which jobs we publish. Please read our Privacy Policy & Disclaimer for details.
