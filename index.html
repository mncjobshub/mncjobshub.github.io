Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find genuine MNC fresher jobs?

Always apply through the company's official careers page. Every job here links to the official apply page. Genuine companies never ask for money for a job, interview or training, so do not pay anyone who asks.

What is an off-campus drive?

An off-campus drive is a hiring event or application process where a company recruits graduates directly, outside college placement programmes. Anyone who meets the eligibility criteria can usually apply online.

Which batches can apply?

Each job page shows the eligible batch or experience level and the education criteria given by the company. If a posting does not state them, check the official page before applying.

Is it free to use this website?

Yes. Browsing jobs and using our links is completely free, and we never charge candidates.

How can I get new job alerts first?

New openings are shared on our Telegram channel and Instagram page as soon as they are published, so joining them is the fastest way to apply early.
